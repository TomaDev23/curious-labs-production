# Motif progression — one journey, not one picture repeated

**Owner, 2026-09-13:** "We are repeating the motif too much without alterations of design… create better continuity
between the different parts and a better progression of the motif versus using the same thing again and again."
Evidence: Earth-at-night appears in the hero, again behind the doors, again in SC-04's header, again in SC-05 "A clearer
tomorrow", again in SC-06's opening and in two reading cards; every band uses the same "headline left + quote top-right"
layout. This file **overrides MOCKUP_CANON and SCENE_SPECS wherever they reuse the Earth or the quote block** (owner
decision). Everything else in the mockup canon stands.

## 1 · The story the page tells with its images

**Orbit → Threshold → Ground → The work → The city → First light → Momentum → Clear air → Return.**
The camera travels from space down to the ground and to people, the night slowly turns to first light, and the page
ends looking *up* at the Earth from the ground — the hero seen from the other side.

## 2 · The continuous thread — the light line

One motif *evolves* from scene to scene instead of an image repeating: **a line of light**.

1. Hero: the atmosphere's rim light on the globe.
2. Doors: the same line becomes an **orbit arc** crossing the void and the light beams on the reflective floor.
3. Walls: it splits into the **three coloured paths** that converge on the walker.
4. Seam SC-03→04 (DR-10): it becomes the **thread** the walker pulls.
5. SC-04: the thread arrives as the **harness connectors** around the core.
6. SC-05: it becomes the **river of city light** and the Cambodia marker line.
7. SC-06: it becomes the **wavy approach path** with the four nodes.
8. SC-07: it becomes the **engagement rail** between the four steps.
9. SC-09: it closes as the **horizon line** under the rising Earth.

Seams between bands: soft gradient dissolves where one scene's light leads into the next. A hard full-width hairline only
at two chapter breaks (after SC-03, after SC-06).

## 3 · Layout rhythm — stop repeating "headline left, quote right"

- **Quote blocks: three on the whole page** — SC-01 hero, SC-05 person, SC-09 final. Remove them from SC-02, SC-04, SC-06,
  SC-07 (their quote strings in COPY_CANON become unused, not moved).
- Header compositions rotate:
  - SC-01 hero: headline left, quote right (the signature).
  - SC-02 doors: headline left, no quote; the orbit arc sweeps across the empty right side.
  - SC-03 walls: headline left, word stack right (as built).
  - SC-04: **centred** header above the centred triptych (symmetry around the core).
  - SC-05: full-bleed photo editorial, headline left over the dark interior, quote right (allowed here).
  - SC-06: **mirrored** — art on the left, headline block right-aligned into columns 6–12.
  - SC-07: compact header left + side text right, the four steps dominate.
  - SC-08: editorial two-column, no art.
  - SC-09: **centred** headline + CTA over the horizon, quote beneath or to the side.
- Reading cards (SC-06) — four different worlds, none a planet horizon: R1 path through terrain (ART-07, keep) · R2 **aerial
  view of many roads converging into one at dawn** (new art-23, replaces the Earth horizon) · R3 orbit-ring planet (ART-10,
  keep — abstract) · R4 **figure on a ledge above a city valley at first light** (new art-24, replaces figure-over-Earth).
- SC-04 left tile: **aerial city street-light grid at night** (new art-25, replaces the Earth-night-lights tile).
