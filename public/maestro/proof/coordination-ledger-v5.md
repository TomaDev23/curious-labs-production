### Mark TYPES
| TYPE | Author | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `OBSERVATION` | either | A noted fact / state / progress note. No action demanded; no ack required. |
| `STEER` | usually `[SHADOW]`/`[SENIOR]` | Advisory course-correction. **Requires an addressed-peer response** (`ACK`+act, or `DEFER`+reason). Advisory, NOT binding. |
| `BLOCKING` | usually `[SHADOW]` | "Do not proceed past this PU until resolved" — a premise-against-source violation, a C1–C16 breach, a guard widened instead of input fixed, or a settled decision silently re-opened. **Requires `ACK`+act or `ESCALATE`.** Unresolved → escalates to operator. |
| `ACK` | the addressed peer | "Seen + acting." Names what changes. Closes the referenced `STEER`/`BLOCKING` once the act lands. |
| `DEFER` | the addressed peer | "Seen, NOT acting now" + reason + when revisited. Legitimate for `STEER`; NOT for `BLOCKING`. |
| `ESCALATE` | either | "This goes to the operator." The operator's ruling returns as a new `OBSERVATION` citing the `ESCALATE` ID. |

## §2 — The steering contract (binding)

1. **Advisory + mandatory-ack.** SHADOW/SENIOR marks are advisory — the EXECUTOR keeps build authority and may `DEFER` a `STEER` with a reason — but the addressed peer MUST respond to every `STEER`/`BLOCKING` directed at it. Silence is a breach.
2. **The response menu is fixed.** `STEER` → {`ACK`+act, `DEFER`+reason, `ESCALATE`}. `BLOCKING` → {`ACK`+act, `ESCALATE`}.
3. **`BLOCKING` halts only its own PU**, not the whole run. Unresolved `BLOCKING` → operator at the executor's next PU-close.
4. **Bidirectional.** The EXECUTOR also writes `STEER`/`OBSERVATION`/`ESCALATE`; **keep the shadow FED** — flag load-bearing premises; the shadow idles without premises.
5. **Cite or it didn't happen.** Premise-level marks carry `file:line` or doc+§. A `BLOCKING` without a cite is downgraded to a `STEER`.
6. **No team-theater (C13).** No numeric pass/fail floors / kill-thresholds / sign-off quorums. Exactly two peers (executor + active shadow) + the sub-agent armies under each; the senior is a lighter-touch continuity/second-voice seat, NOT a review board.
